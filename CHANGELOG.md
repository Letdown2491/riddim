# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release.

- Search tracks and playlists, browse artist and album pages, pin playlists
  to the sidebar.
- Play locally with queue, history, and radio, driven by Spotify's Soloist
  daemon, which Riddim downloads and keeps updated.
- Now Playing and media keys through MPRIS; light, dark, and system
  appearances; persistent playback and library state.
- Requires Spotify Premium and a one-time setup (your own Spotify developer
  app, then pairing the Riddim playback device); the walkthrough is in
  `docs/spotify-setup.md`.
- Linux (x86_64, aarch64), distributed as a Flatpak and published through
  [Orchard](https://github.com/Letdown2491/orchard).
